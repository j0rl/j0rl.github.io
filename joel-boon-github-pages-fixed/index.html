Aircraft wing

A wing built for a 97 lbf end load, then tested to a final load of 300 lbf.

With my team in Engineering Design, I designed, manufactured, and tested the wing. I used static and buckling finite element analysis to refine bulkhead number and spacing before fabrication.

02 MECHANICAL DESIGN / TEAM PROJECT

Autonomous search-and-rescue robot

A lifting mechanism designed to move a 20 lb companion robot across a barrier.

My sub-team designed and built the arm, sliding carriage, and winch for a two-robot rescue system. I modeled parts in PTC Creo, used FEA, manufactured components, and helped with testing and debugging. The mechanism successfully lifted and translated the smaller robot.

03 SPACE SYSTEMS / TEAM PROJECT

VAMPS mission design

A three-spacecraft study of the Van Allen belt environment.

On the guidance, navigation, and attitude determination and control sub-team, I analyzed subsystem needs, wrote requirements, and coordinated across teams for a NASA-style Phase A mission study. Our ADC poster documents disturbance torque estimates, hardware choices, budgeting, and resonance analysis of the sensor booms.

MAE 342 · Space System DesignJan–May 2026
